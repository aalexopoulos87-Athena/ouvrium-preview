Earlier releases are in the git history (v1 8354ec2, v2 0094c5b, build-your-own 7ecaaf1). Pages are generated by

## Standing rule: all spotlight / banner art pops out of the frame

From 20 Nov 2026 this is a standing Ouvrium rule, not a one-off Friday tweak. Every homepage daily
spotlight and every matching banner/hero that uses the gold-frame `.sp` treatment must have the figure
break past the frame (top and edge), the same way the live Mon / Tue / Wed / Sat / Sun spotlights do.
Technique is CSS only: `overflow:visible` on the panel and `.sp-art`, wrap shifted up
(`top:-72px; height:112%; bottom:-6%` on desktop; `top:-52px; height:118%` / `top:-44px; height:120%`
on smaller screens, or Saturday's taller `top:-88px` when the subject sits at the top of the asset),
soft mask fade in the overflow that is solid by the gold edge, same layering and shadows. Art pixels
are not redrawn. New or replaced spotlight art must ship with this treatment; a rebuild must keep it.
