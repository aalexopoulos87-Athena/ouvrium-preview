## 2026-11-16: store credit wallet, pay with credit, shop singles (preview)

Backup before editing: `/workspace/ouvrium-site/backups/2026-11-16-credit-*` (src/*.py, site/assets, buyback/vault/index HTML).

### New files
- `assets/credit.js`: demo store-credit wallet (localStorage `ouv_credit`, history `ouv_credit_hist`); credit is earned only
  from 90% buybacks; `O.creditEarn` / `O.creditSpend`; header wallet badge (`#walletBadge`, in the nav menu); `O.payPanel`
  (pay-with-credit panel: pay at checkout / pay with credit / use part of my credit, with a slider).
- `assets/shop.js`: /shop/ UI: game chips, price band, search, sort, stock counts, "Buy with credit" into a cart, pickup in
  Ottawa (free) or tracked shipping ($9.99 demo placeholder), credit slider, demo checkout and order confirmation. Stock
  reservations (`ouv_shop_sold`), orders (`ouv_orders`), cart (`ouv_cart`) stay in the browser.
- `assets/shop-data.js`: 5,390 in-stock singles at $1+ from the five themed games, Carta Magica Ottawa export 10 Nov 2026
  (name, set, game, shelf price, real quantity, product id).
- `assets/creditpage.js`: /credit/ wallet balance, history table, "Demo: sell back a sample pull".
- `shop/index.html`: shop page (EN/FR, mobile bottom-sheet cart).
- `credit/index.html`: how store credit works: earning, the two spend paths, rules (draft, pending legal review),
  why credit is never cash (s.206 note), competitor comparison with sources.
- `CHANGES.md`: this file.

### Edited files (additive only)
- `assets/reveal.js`: adds `#paybox` under every pack stage (themed, core and builder packs) via `O.payPanel`; credit is
  debited when the pack is opened (tear or Skip); panel re-arms on "Reset demo"; buyback now writes a history line and
  shows "Spend your credit" links. Odds and draw are unchanged.
- `assets/style.css`: appended styles for the wallet badge, pay panel, shop and cart sheet; tighter nav spacing at
  901–1200px so the extra Shop link and badge fit without horizontal scroll.
- `assets/vault.js`: "Clear all demo data" also clears the new credit/cart/order keys.
- All generated pages: nav gains "Shop"; footer Trust column gains "Store credit" and "Shop singles"; every page loads
  `assets/credit.js` after `gates.js`.
- `index.html`: extra "Or shop singles with credit" button. `buyback.html`: "Spend it two ways" callout linking to
  shop/ and credit/. `vault.html`: link to credit balance/history and shop.

### Unchanged
Preview banner, noindex, no payment fields, waitlist disabled, fan-made disclaimers, original art only, published odds = draw odds.

## 2026-11-16 (b): shop modelled on the Carta Magica storefront preview

Read-only reuse from `/workspace/cm-art` (Carta Magica storefront preview repo; not modified):
- Card-image matching: `data/ottawa-singles/{pokemon,one-piece,yugioh,gundam,union-arena}.json` maps Crystal Commerce
  Product ID → public card-image URL. 5,255 of 5,390 shop singles now show card art (hotlinked like the CM site,

Gundam images from gundam-gcg.com send `Cross-Origin-Resource-Policy: same-site`, so those 119 cards use the fallback.

- New: `assets/cardback-ouvrium.svg` (original fallback art).
- Edited: `assets/shop-data.js` (image host table + path per card, set lists), `assets/shop.js` (art tiles, set filter,
  chip counts), `assets/style.css` (tile grid, hover lift; no motion under reduced-motion), `shop/index.html` (set select;
